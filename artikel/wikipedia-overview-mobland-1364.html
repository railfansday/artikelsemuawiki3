Topic Description: 2025 British crime drama television series

MobLand is a crime drama television series created by Ronan Bennett for Paramount+. The series stars Tom Hardy as Harry Da Souza, Pierce Brosnan as Conrad Harrigan and Helen Mirren as Maeve Harrigan.

Additional main characters include Kevin Harrigan, Jan Da Souza, Isabella Harrigan, Eddie Harrigan, and Seraphina Harrigan. Guy Ritchie, Anthony Byrne, Daniel Syrkin, and Lawrence Gough serve as directors.

Jez Butterworth and Bennett are credited with writing all ten episodes of the first season.
