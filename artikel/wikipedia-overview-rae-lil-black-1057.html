Topic Description: Japanese influencer (born 1995/1996)

Kae Asakura, formerly known professionally as Rae Lil Black, is a Japanese social media influencer, content creator, and former pornographic film actress. She shares vlogs on platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.

Following a visit to Malaysia in 2024, she converted to Islam.
