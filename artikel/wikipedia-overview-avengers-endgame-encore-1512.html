Topic Description: 2026 Marvel Studios film re-release

Avengers Endgame: Encore is the 2026 re-release of Avengers: Endgame, a 2019 American superhero film produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. Endgame was first re-released in June 2019, during its original theatrical run.

A new re-release was revealed in December 2025 and the Encore subtitle was announced in June 2026. It features new footage that serves as a narrative bridge between Endgame and its sequel, Avengers: Doomsday (2026).

This footage was filmed by Endgame and Doomsday directors Anthony and Joe Russo during Doomsday's production, and appears exclusively in Encore at the end of the original film and through its credits.
