Topic Description: Lebanese-American actress (born 2003)

Sara Boustany is a Lebanese-American actress. She began her television career on Pen15 (2019) and Dolly Parton's Heartstrings (2019), and made her feature film debut in Hello, Beautiful (2025).

She gained wide recognition for her breakout starring role as Elise Zakaria in the Netflix coming-of-age thriller series The Body (2026).
