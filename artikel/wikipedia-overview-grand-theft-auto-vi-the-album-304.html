Topic Description: 2026 soundtrack album by various artists

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is a soundtrack album for the 2026 action-adventure video game Grand Theft Auto VI. It was released on November 20, 2026, through Atlantic Records and Take-Two Interactive.

The album, which emulates the music of Florida within Grand Theft Auto VI's fictional setting of Leonida, features 34 original tracks on the standard edition from a diverse ensemble of artists including Yung Lean, Cardi B, Travis Scott, Lana Del Rey, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred Again, Zara Larsson, Dua Lipa and Morgan Wallen, among others.
