Topic Description: American actress and filmmaker (born 1974)

Elizabeth Banks is an American actress and filmmaker. She is known for playing Effie Trinket in The Hunger Games film series (2012–2015) and an ICCA commentator in the Pitch Perfect film series (2012–2017).

She made her directorial film debut with Pitch Perfect 2 (2015), whose $69 million opening-weekend gross set a record for a first-time director. She directed the action comedy Charlie's Angels (2019) and the black comedy horror film Cocaine Bear (2023).

Banks founded the film and television production company Brownstone Productions in 2002 with her husband, Max Handelman.
