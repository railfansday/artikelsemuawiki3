Topic Description: 1954–1955 fantasy novel by J. R. R. Tolkien

The Lord of the Rings is a high fantasy novel written by the English author and scholar J. R.

R. Tolkien.

Set in Middle-earth, the epic began as a sequel to Tolkien's 1937 children's book The Hobbit but eventually developed into a much larger work. Written in stages between 1937 and 1949, The Lord of the Rings is one of the best-selling books ever written, with over 150 million copies sold.
