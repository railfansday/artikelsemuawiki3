Topic Description: American dancer, choreographer, actor, singer (born 1985)

Derek Bruce Hough is an American professional Latin and ballroom dancer, choreographer, singer, and television personality. He is best known for his appearances on the ABC competition series Dancing with the Stars, where he performed as a pro for seventeen seasons and won the series a record-breaking six times with his celebrity partners.

For his work, Hough won four Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Choreography, and was nominated for the award fourteen times. He later became a judge on the series, beginning with its twenty-ninth season.

He also currently hosts the entertainment news program Extra.
