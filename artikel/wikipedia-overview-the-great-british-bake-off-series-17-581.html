Topic Description: Seventeenth series of The Great British Bake Off

The seventeenth series of The Great British Bake Off began on 22 September 2026 and was presented by Alison Hammond and Noel Fielding. Paul Hollywood returned as a judge, alongside Nigella Lawson, who replaced Prue Leith following her departure.
