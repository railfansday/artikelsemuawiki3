Topic Description: English actor (born 1980)

Benjamin John Whishaw is an English actor. He is best known for roles in TV series The Hour (2011–12), This Is Going to Hurt (2022), and the Netflix spy thriller series Black Doves (2024–present).

He played Q in the James Bond films Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015), and No Time to Die (2021). For his portrayal of Norman Scott in the miniseries A Very English Scandal (2018) he won a BAFTA, a Golden Globe Award and a Primetime Emmy Award for Best Supporting Actor.
