Topic Description: American professional wrestler (born 1969)

Dustin Patrick Runnels is an American professional wrestler. He is signed to All Elite Wrestling (AEW), where he performs under the ring name Dustin Rhodes and is a former AEW TNT Champion.

He also appears in AEW's sister promotion Ring of Honor (ROH), where he is a member of the Sons of Texas stable. In ROH, Rhodes is a former ROH World Six-Man Tag Team Champion and ROH World Tag Team Champion.
