Topic Description: Generative AI chatbot by OpenAI

ChatGPT is a generative artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot developed by OpenAI. It uses large language models – specifically generative pre-trained transformers (GPTs) – to generate text in response to user prompts.

ChatGPT popularized the use of generative AI and accelerated the ongoing AI boom, a period marked by rapid investment and public attention toward AI. OpenAI operates the service on a freemium model.
