Topic Description: American actor (born 1953)

John Gavin Malkovich is an American actor. Known for his performances as eccentric and amoral characters, his accolades include three Emmy Awards and a Volpi Cup, with nominations for two Academy Awards, a BAFTA Award, three Golden Globes, and a Laurence Olivier Award.
