Topic Description: Former president and COO of Theranos (born 1965)

Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani is a businessman who was the former president and chief operating officer of Theranos, which was a privately held health technology company founded by his then-girlfriend Elizabeth Holmes. He and Holmes fraudulently represented that they had devised a revolutionary blood test that required only small amounts of blood, such as from a fingerstick.

Starting in 2015, Theranos came under criticism in the media due to its questionable claims and practices. The consequences of the fraud led to the collapse of Theranos, while private investors lost $700M to $945M in real money.

The company was eventually liquidated.
