Topic Description: Ongoing armed conflict in West Asia

Since 28 February 2026, the United States and Israel have been at war with Iran and its regional allies, following the Twelve-Day War in June 2025 and sporadic fighting during the Middle Eastern crisis sparked by the Gaza war, which began in 2023. Amid ongoing US–Iran negotiations regarding Iran's nuclear program, the war began when the US and Israel conducted hundreds of air strikes on targets across Iran, including strikes that assassinated Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei alongside several other Iranian officials.

Iran responded by launching missiles and drones against countries in the Middle East and blocking trade through the Strait of Hormuz, resulting in an unprecedented global fuel crisis, high inflation and a surge in the cost of living worldwide.
