Topic Description: 2026 Indian film by Nitesh Tiwari

Ramayana: Part 1 is a 2026 Indian Hindi-language epic mythological film directed by Nitesh Tiwari, who co-wrote the screenplay with Shridhar Raghavan. Produced by Namit Malhotra and Yash, the film is based on the Hindu epic Ramayana by Valmiki.

It is the first instalment of a two-part film series, with Part 1 being based on the first three kāṇḍa (chapters) of Ramayana, which chronicle the life of Prince Rama from childhood up to his adventurous 14-year exile. The first part features an ensemble cast that includes Ranbir Kapoor, Yash, Sai Pallavi, Ravi Dubey, Anupam Kher, Lara Dutta, Shobana, Kajal Aggarwal, Rakul Preet Singh, Vivek Oberoi, Kunal Kapoor, Arun Govil, Adinath Kothare.
