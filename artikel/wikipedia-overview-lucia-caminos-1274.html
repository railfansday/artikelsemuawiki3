Topic Description: Fictional character in Grand Theft Auto VI

Lucia Maria Rivera Caminos is a fictional character and one of two playable protagonists, alongside her boyfriend, Jason Duval, in Rockstar Games's 2026 video game Grand Theft Auto VI. Portrayed by Manni L.

Perez, she is the first non-optional playable female protagonist in the Grand Theft Auto series. Originally from Liberty City, Liberty State, she is a skilled fighter who grew up being taught by her father.

She is dedicated to achieving the "good life her mom has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City", and thus begins engaging in criminal activities in Liberty City and Vice City, Leonida, but is subsequently arrested and placed into the Leonida Penitentiary. She is later released on parole with an ankle monitor, but after performing more planned heists and criminal activities alongside crewmate and romantic interest Jason Duval, the two begin discovering a state-wide criminal conspiracy, requiring them to protect each other to stay alive.
