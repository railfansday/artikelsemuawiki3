Topic Description: Season of television series

Bigg Boss 10, also known as Bigg Boss: Carnivizha is the tenth season of the Indian Tamil-language reality television series Bigg Boss, produced by Banijay. Vijay Sethupathi is returning as a host for the third consecutive year.

Ramya Krishnan as special host in week 7 for the second time after fifth season.
