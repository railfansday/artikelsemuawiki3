Topic Description: Indian actress (born 1992)

Sai Pallavi Senthamarai Kannan is an Indian actress who primarily works in Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam films. Known for her natural, girl-next-door screen presence and for portraying women in emotionally challenging circumstances, she has received several accolades, including a Tamil Nadu State Film Award, seven Filmfare Awards South, and three SIIMA Awards.

One of South India's highest-paid actresses, she was featured in Forbes India's 2020 list of 30 Under 30 and was honoured with the Kalaimamani award by the Government of Tamil Nadu.
