Topic Description: House elections for the 120th U.S. Congress

The 2026 United States House of Representatives elections were held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, as part of the 2026 midterm elections during President Donald Trump's second, nonconsecutive term. Voters elected representatives from all 435 congressional districts across each of the fifty U.S.

states, and five of the six non-voting delegates from the District of Columbia and the inhabited U.S. territories.
