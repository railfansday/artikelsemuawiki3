Topic Description: Regular-season tournament of the National Basketball Association

The NBA Cup is an annual National Basketball Association (NBA) tournament during the regular season. It was officially announced on July 8, 2023, and debuted during the 2023–24 NBA season.

The first edition of the event was called the In-Season Tournament.
